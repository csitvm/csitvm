About CSI and the Trivandrum Chapter

Who we are and how the chapter began.

About CSI

Computer Society of India (CSI) is the first, largest and most professionally managed association of and for IT professionals in India. CSI is a non-profit organization established in 1965, and its members are committed to the advancement of theory and practice of Computer Engineering and Technology Systems. The society celebrated its Golden Jubilee in 2015.

About CSI Trivandrum

The activities of the Computer Society of India started in the city of Trivandrum with the annual convention CSI 69 in January 1969, on the theme "Training in Software".

Sri. J.T. George and his colleagues of the Thumba Equatorial Rocket Launching Station led this event. With a membership of over forty, the 11th chapter of CSI came into being in 1976, with Dr. D.S. Rane, then Head of the Computer Division of VSSC, as its first Chairman. Mr. K. Thanappan Nair of the Government Data Processing Centre was the founder Secretary of the Chapter. The Chapter was officially inaugurated on 25 June 1976 by Sri. F.C. Kohli, then President of CSI and the founder of TCS, who passed away in 2020.

With financial assistance from CSI Headquarters, the Trivandrum Chapter acquired office premises consisting of an office room and a lecture hall in the heart of the city. The premises are equipped with the infrastructure needed for its training programmes.
