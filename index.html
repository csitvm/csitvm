Welcome to CSI Trivandrum

Computer Society of India (CSI) is the first, largest and most professionally managed association of and for IT professionals in India. The Trivandrum Chapter, the 11th chapter of CSI, brings the profession together through talks, training, publications and a student council.

Why be a member?

CSI members benefit from taking part in professional activities conducted by CSI, and receive CSI publications: CSI Communications, CSI Journal of Computing and CSI Transactions on ICT. Members can also grow and contribute by taking up leadership roles in chapters, and at regional and national level.
