Membership

Join CSI and take part in the chapter's professional activities.

Why be a member?

CSI members benefit from taking part in professional activities conducted by CSI, and receive CSI publications: CSI Communications, CSI Journal of Computing and CSI Transactions on ICT. Members can also grow and contribute by taking up leadership roles in chapters, and at regional and national level.

Questions about membership?

Write to the chapter office. Contact details are on the Contact page.
